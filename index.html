About the hub

One chair, one set of clippers, and a habit of sending people out looking sharper than they walked in. Osulwe's Hair Hub started as a side hustle to fund the books — it's grown into the cut people trust before an interview, a night out, or just a Monday. Chiskops, fades, taper cuts: whatever you're after, it's done properly, every time.

Based in Summerstrand, the door's open to anyone in the area — students, staff, families, whoever's next in the chair. Bring a photo, describe the vibe, or just trust the clippers.
